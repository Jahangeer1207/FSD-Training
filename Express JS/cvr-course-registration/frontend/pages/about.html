About CVR College of Engineering

Empowering students through quality education and innovation.

Institute History

CVR College of Engineering was established with the vision of providing world-class technical education and producing highly skilled engineers. The institution has earned recognition for academic excellence, industry-focused learning, and outstanding placement opportunities.

The college was granted autonomous status by the UGC and continues to strengthen its reputation through quality teaching, research, and innovation across multiple engineering disciplines.

Vision

To become a leading institution in engineering and technology education by nurturing innovation, research, leadership, and ethical values.

Mission

  • Deliver quality technical education.
  • Promote research and innovation.
  • Develop industry-ready professionals.
  • Encourage ethical and social responsibility.
