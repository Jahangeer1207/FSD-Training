About Us

Learn more about our institution, vision, mission, and faculty.

Institute History

CVR College of Engineering was established by The Cherabuddi Education Society in 1999. The institution was founded with the objective of providing high-quality technical education and producing skilled professionals capable of meeting industry demands. Over the years, CVR has become one of the leading engineering colleges, known for academic excellence and innovative teaching methods.

Vision

To become a center of excellence in education, research, and innovation by empowering students with knowledge, skills, and values required to succeed globally.

Mission

  • Provide quality education using modern teaching methods.
  • Promote innovation, research, and entrepreneurship.
  • Develop industry-ready professionals.
  • Encourage ethical values and lifelong learning.
