CVR College of Engineering

The Cherabuddi Education Society was registered in January 1999 as an NRI-promoted society. The Society aims at creating a state-of-the-art engineering institution in association with leading NRI technology professionals and well-known academicians of Telangana and Andhra Pradesh. Our students remain at the heart of our vision, benefiting from innovative teaching methods and close links with premier companies and research institutions in India and abroad.
